> **Status:** DERIVED transcription. The referral-program PDF remains authoritative. Preserve the source document's distinction between policy and sections labeled “Recommended.”

## Public terms

- Referral rewards are issued as account credit only.
- Credits have no cash value and are non-transferable.
- Credit is issued after the referred customer completes the first paid service.
- If the referred customer cancels before completing the first paid service, no credit is applied.
- If the referring customer cancels service, unused referral credits are forfeited.
- Credits cannot be redeemed or exchanged for cash.

## Internal office policy

- Apply referral credit only after the first invoice is paid.
- Verify the referral source in the CRM before applying credit.
- Add credit using the designated `Referral Credit` service code.
- No manual cash payouts.
- If the account cancels, unused credit is immediately void.
- Credits may not be transferred between accounts without management approval.
